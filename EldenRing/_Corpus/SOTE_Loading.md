## Scadutree Blessing [80]

Consume Scadutree Fragments at sites of grace to bolster your Scadutree Blessing.

The Scadutree Blessing bolsters the recipient's abilities to deal and negate damage, but has no influence outside the realm of shadow.

## Revered Spirit Ash Blessing [81]

Consume Revered Spirit Ash at sites of grace to bolster your Revered Spirit Ash Blessing.

The Revered Spirit Ash Blessing bolsters both summoned spirits' and spectral steed's abilities to deal and negate damage but has no influence outside the realm of shadow.

## Carrying Over Summoning Pools [207]

You can enable or disable summoning pools individually using the list on the map functions menu.
