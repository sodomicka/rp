## Designations et datation

Le corpus nomme la nuit "Night of the Black Knives" (_Corpus/ER_Goods_02.md, Black Knifeprint),
"the night of the dire plot" (_Corpus/ER_Goods_01.md, Blasphemous Claw) et "that fateful night"
(_Corpus/ER_Weapons_01.md, Maliketh's Black Blade).

Datation : "It happened during the Golden Age of the Erdtree, long before the shattering of the
Elden Ring." ([325022020]) La narration d'ouverture : "On a night of wint'ry fog."
(_Corpus/ER_Talk_01.md, [16000200]).

Chaine causale : "And it became the catalyst. Soon, the Elden Ring was smashed, and thus sprang
forth the war known as the Shattering." ([325022060]) Meme ordre en ouverture, avec un maillon
de plus : "Queen Marika was driven to the brink." ([16000500]) Une variante laisse la cause
ouverte - "The Elden Ring was broken, but by whom? And why?" ([16500700]). [INCERTAIN]

## Le vol et le rite

"On the Night of the Black Knives, someone stole a fragment of Death from Maliketh, the Black
Blade, and imbued its power into the assassins' daggers." (Black Knifeprint) Ni cette entree ni
Rogier a ce stade ne nomment le voleur ([325022030]).

Le vol ne suffit pas : "A ritual performed on the oddly misshapen blade imbued it with the
power of the stolen Rune of Death." (_Corpus/ER_Weapons_01.md, Black Knife) Ranni revendique la
forge : "I stole a fragment of the Rune of Death, and used it to forge the godslaying black
knives through fearsome rite." (_Corpus/ER_Talk_01.md, [106110020])

Maliketh scelle ensuite sa lame dans sa propre chair (_Corpus/ER_Weapons_01.md, Maliketh's
Black Blade ; cf. Destined_Death.md).

## Les executantes

"The assassins that carried out the deeds of the Night of the Black Knives were all women, and
rumored to be Numen who had close ties with Marika herself." (_Corpus/ER_Armor_01.md, Black
Knife Hood) Reserve du texte source. [INCERTAIN]

Rogier en donne une autre, aussi rapportee : "They say the assassins who carried out the deed
were scions of the Eternal City." ([325023030]) Il les decrit "arrayed in armour of silver
under cloaks which fooled the eye" ([325023040]). Le corpus ne concilie jamais les deux - cf.
TROU 2.

Deux sont nommees : Alecto, Black Knife Ringleader (_Corpus/ER_Npcs.md, [902100521]) et sa
fille - "Tiche was one of the assassins who, on the night of the plot, imbued her black knife
with the Rune of Death and slew Godwyn the Golden. She was the daughter of the Black Knife
Ringleader, Alecto, and was killed protecting her mother during their flight from the royal
capital." (_Corpus/ER_Goods_04.md, Black Knife Tiche)

## Godwyn, la double mort, la cursemark

"Dagger once belonging to one of the assassins who murdered Godwyn the Golden on the Night of
the Black Knives." (Black Knife) ; "That was the first recorded Death of a demigod in all
history." ([325022050])

"This cursemark was carved at the moment of Death of the first demigod, and should have taken
the shape of a circle." puis "However, two demigods perished at the same time, breaking the
cursemark into two half-wheels." puis "Ranni was the first of the demigods whose flesh
perished, while the Prince of Death perished in soul alone." (_Corpus/ER_Goods_02.md, Cursemark
of Death)

Version de Ranni : "I stole the Rune of Death, slew mine own Empyrean flesh, casting it away."
([106030050]) ; "And it is upon that flesh the cursemark is carved." ([106110060]) Rogier
confirme la separation : "When Ranni shed her flesh, she shed the cursemark, too."
([325040010])

Cursemark et hallowbrand designent la meme figure : la Cursemark of Death est "Also known as
the half-wheel wound of the centipede." et Fia dit "When the first of the demigods died, his
flesh was marked with the half-wheel wound of the centipede." ([322051010]) ; "Godwyn's
hallowbrand has since been recovered at the Roundtable Hold." ([322051020])

## Motif revendique et complicites

Ranni rattache le vol a son refus de succession : "But I would not acquiesce to the Two
Fingers." ([106030040]), puis "I would not be controlled by that thing." ([106030060]) Elle
revendique le tout : "I did it all." ([106110030])

Rogier arrive au meme nom par l'empreinte : "The person who orchestrated the Night of the Black
Knives." puis "Lunar Princess Ranni." ([325030020], [325030030]) - "Her's was the name I
discovered in the imprint." ([325030050])

Rykard fut arme le soir meme : "On the night of the dire plot, Ranni rewarded Praetor Rykard
with these traces. Should the coming trespass one day transpire, they would serve as a
last-resort foil, allowing Rykard to challenge Maliketh the Black Blade, the black beast of
Destined Death." (_Corpus/ER_Goods_01.md, Blasphemous Claw)

## Ce que le corpus declare cache

"This mark is evidence of the ritual, and hides the truth of the conspiracy." (Black
Knifeprint) Fia le redit ([322023050]). Rogier annonce une decouverte qu'il n'enonce jamais :
"but I discovered something in my examination of the Night of the Black Knives." ([325033060]) ;
"These souls have committed no offence. They have every right to life, only, they happened to
touch upon a flaw in the Order." ([325033070])

  Later, the Rune of Death spread across the Lands Between through the underground roots of the

## Trous et contraintes de comblement

### TROU 1 - Que cache le Knifeprint, et qu'a decouvert Rogier ?
- DIT : le Knifeprint "hides the truth of the conspiracy" ; Rogier a decouvert "something" et
  lie Those Who Live in Death a "a flaw in the Order".
- NON DIT : la verite cachee, et le contenu de la decouverte. Rogier ne l'enonce jamais.
- CONTRAINTES : (a) l'aveu de Ranni est explicite, aucun comblement ne peut la disculper du vol
  ni du rite ; (b) le texte affirme lui-meme qu'une verite demeure cachee, donc cet aveu ne peut
  pas etre l'explication complete ; (c) la decouverte de Rogier porte sur le STATUT de Those Who
  Live in Death, pas seulement sur l'identite d'un commanditaire.
- RENVOI : Systemes/Destined_Death.md, TROU 2.

### TROU 2 - D'ou viennent les assassines ?
- DIT : toutes des femmes ; deux origines rapportees, "Numen who had close ties with Marika
  herself" et "scions of the Eternal City" (cf. supra) ; Alecto et Tiche sont nommees ;
  la fuite se fait hors de la capitale royale.
- NON DIT : la conciliation des deux origines, et le recrutement. Aucun texte ne les rapproche
  ni ne les oppose.
- CONTRAINTES : (a) les DEUX enonces sont rapportes, aucun n'est affirme - un comblement ne peut
  promouvoir l'un en fait sans decision du worldbuilder ; (b) une origine Numen les apparente a
  Marika, une origine Eternal City les apparente aux cites de Nokron et Nokstella nommees par le
  corpus (_Corpus/ER_Places.md) : le corpus ne declare pas les deux incompatibles ; (c) la
  filiation Alecto -> Tiche est etablie et doit tenir.

### TROU 3 - Laquelle des deux morts etait visee ?
- DIT : la cursemark "should have taken the shape of a circle" et fut brisee parce que "two
  demigods perished at the same time" ; Ranni perd la chair, le Prince of Death perd l'ame.
- NON DIT : quelle mort etait prevue, et pourquoi les deux coincident. Le corpus ne pose jamais
  la question.
- CONTRAINTES : (a) une seule mort etait attendue, la forme circulaire le suppose ; (b) la
  simultaneite est un fait enonce, pas une lecture ; (c) tension de vocabulaire a preserver :
  Godwyn est "first of the demigods to die" (_Corpus/ER_Weapons_01.md, Golden Epitaph) tandis
  que Ranni est "the first of the demigods whose flesh perished" (Cursemark of Death).

### TROU 4 - Le deroulement materiel de la nuit
- DIT : des lames, des voiles qui trompent l'oeil, une armure "forged to make no sound"
  (_Corpus/ER_Armor_01.md, Black Knife Armor), une fuite hors de la capitale royale, et "The
  misshapen corpse under Stormveil" ([325022000]), dont Rogier dit "a sacred relic. Of the
  black knives plot." ([325022010]).
- NON DIT : le lieu de l'attaque, son heure, le nombre d'assassines, la presence ou l'absence de
  Marika ou de Maliketh. Le corpus ne narre jamais la nuit elle-meme.
- CONTRAINTES : (a) la capitale royale est le point de FUITE ; le corpus n'en fait pas
  explicitement le lieu du meurtre ; (b) l'infiltration est furtive, et le pouvoir y repond par
  une torche revelant "assassins cloaked in veils" ; (c) tout comblement doit laisser Maliketh
  survivant et depossede d'un seul fragment.

### TROU 5 - Pourquoi Ranni prevoyait-elle un affrontement Rykard / Maliketh ?
- DIT : elle recompense Rykard le soir meme, en prevision de "the coming trespass".
- NON DIT : la nature de cette transgression a venir, et si Rykard fut complice de la nuit ou
  seulement dote apres coup.
- CONTRAINTES : (a) le don est date de la nuit du complot ; (b) il vise Maliketh nommement, donc
  Ranni anticipait que le gardien resterait un obstacle apres le vol ; (c) l'objet est un
  "last-resort foil", pas un moyen de tuer.
